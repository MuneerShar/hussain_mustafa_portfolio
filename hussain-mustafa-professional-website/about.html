INTRODUCTION

Engineering with a focus on better processes.

I am a final-semester Civil Engineering student at Aror University of Art, Architecture, Design and Heritage, Sukkur. My academic interests centre on construction management, sustainable construction, material efficiency and reducing waste on building projects.

My current research explores construction waste management through Lean practices, integrating Value Stream Mapping (VSM) and Waste Tracking (WT) in building projects across Sindh, Pakistan. I am interested in understanding how practical methods can help identify inefficiencies and improve everyday construction activities.

My internship at the Girls’ Hostel construction project at Begum Nusrat Bhutto Women University gave me exposure to site activities including PCC, RCC, reinforcement, masonry, plastering, formwork and concrete work.
