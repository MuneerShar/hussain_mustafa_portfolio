RESEARCH OBJECTIVES

What the research aims to explore.

01

Develop a practical framework

Manage and reduce waste in construction projects using Lean practices.

02

Map the process

Use Value Stream Mapping (VSM) to identify delays, unnecessary activities and inefficiencies in construction processes.

03

Track material waste

Apply Waste Tracking (WT) to monitor construction waste and understand where materials are being wasted.

04

Improve daily activities

Explore ways to reduce material waste, avoid unnecessary costs and improve daily construction activities.

05

Support sustainability

Promote better use of resources and support more efficient and sustainable construction practices.
